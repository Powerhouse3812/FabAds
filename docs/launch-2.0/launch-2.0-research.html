In plain terms

What is "Launch 2.0"?

A faster, guided way to launch lots of Meta ads at once. Instead of filling every field by hand, the user picks a ready-made strategy.

1 · Pick a
Strategy
e.g. 1:5:1		 → 2 · Add

Goal: fewer inputs, stronger validation, fewer mistakes. Advanced users can still build "from scratch" — but the aim is they rarely need to.

The problem we're solving

Why now — 3 real problems

1 · BROKEN   Today's launch doesn't actually publish — the execution is a stub that fakes "Launch successful!" Catalogue & Fast-Launch don't execute at all.
2 · CONFUSING   It's a long, multi-step, back-and-forth flow. Validation is scattered & skippable → users hit errors after launching → bad experience → they may not retry. (Neeraj's point.)
3 · INCOMPLETE   Half of Meta's fields are missing — custom audiences, lookalikes, "use existing post", real pixel/conversion, and more. Current objectives are even stale (Meta changed them).

The core concept

What a "Launch Strategy" is

A strategy = a structure written as campaign : ad set : ad.

RecipeMeansIt isolates…
1 : 5 : 11 campaign · 5 ad sets · 1 ad eachAudience test (same ad, 5 audiences)
1 : 1 : 51 campaign · 1 ad set · 5 adsCreative test (1 audience, 5 ads)
1 : 3 : 51 campaign · 3 ad sets · 5 adsBoth
"Bruno"1000s of cheap ads, tiny budgetsMass-test to find winners name unconfirmed

Important finding: all named recipes are TEST shapes — there's no named scaling recipe (and scaling is out of Launch's scope anyway). "Bruno" only works across many Pages because of the 250-cap.

How creatives fill the structure

Creative mapping — 3 modes

ModeWhat it doesBest for
Distribute across adsSpread each media item across the ad slots, repeating as needed.Fast experiments
Multiply by mediaClone the whole structure once per creative (5 media → 5× the structure).Clean per-creative read
Map manuallyDrag exact creatives into exact slots.Hand-crafted launches
Finding   In the prototype these 3 aren't really distinct — "Manual" is just "Distribute" + overrides, and "Multiply" has a math bug. Neeraj asked "are these the same?" — basically yes, today. 2.0 must make them genuinely distinct (or collapse to 2 + an override) and show creative thumbnails (currently filename dropdowns only).

What 2.0 must bake in

Fields & constraints

All Meta fields — incl. the ones v1 missed: custom audiences, lookalikes, saved audiences, post-ID / existing post, real pixel + conversion + attribution, interests/behaviors targeting, formats (carousel/collection/flexible), dayparting. (Team callouts: post-ID, custom audience, static/flexible, catalogue-partnership.)
The 250-ads-per-Page cap — Meta caps active ads per Page, aggregated across all ad accounts. This is exactly why distribution across (account, page) pairs matters — it's our differentiator and a hard rule the builder must respect.
Scale — bulk means thousands of rows. Needs table virtualization + server-side batched execution + real page-capacity data (today it's mock).
