Launch · Step 3 · Ad & Distribution

Errors & the right fix for each

When a launch won't fit, the fix depends on why. The big one: “Add a Page” only helps when ads can spill onto it — everywhere else it's a replace, remove, or switch.

✦

The Add-a-Page rule. Adding a Page works only when ads can be redistributed to it — that's Fill-first (and, as a backup, Equal). With Duplicate, One-page, or a Page that's already full, a new Page changes nothing — swap it, remove it, or change how ads spread.
